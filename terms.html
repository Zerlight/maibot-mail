MaiBot Mail is a personal integration operated by its account holder. It submits search requests by email and retrieves matching replies for MaiBot. It is not a public mailbox hosting service.

Connect only accounts you own or are authorized to manage. Keep OAuth credentials private. Use the integration in accordance with Google’s terms and the terms of the configured search and model services.

Search results come from an external service and may be incomplete or outdated. Delivery and availability depend on Gmail, the search service, and the operator’s running MaiBot instance. This integration does not promise continuous availability or a particular search outcome.

The integration does not collect payments. Connected services have their own terms, quotas, and account arrangements. The account holder can stop the integration and revoke its Google access at any time.
