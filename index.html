MaiBot Mail

A personal email integration for MaiBot.

The integration submits search queries to a configured email search service, retrieves the matching replies, and makes the results available to MaiBot.

Google account access

The account holder authorizes Gmail read-only access and permission to send email. The integration uses these permissions to send search requests and retrieve their replies. It does not delete messages or change Gmail labels.

This integration is operated for personal use. Access to a Google account requires that account holder’s authorization.
