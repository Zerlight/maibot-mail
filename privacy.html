MaiBot Mail

Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026.

Information processed

MaiBot Mail processes the authorized Gmail address, OAuth access and refresh tokens, search queries and optional notes, request identifiers, and email replies associated with those requests. Gmail read-only access permits mailbox reading; the integration retrieves replies matching its request identifiers and checks the reply sender.

Purpose and sharing

Queries and notes are sent by email to the configured search service. The sender address is visible to that service. Matching reply content is returned to MaiBot and may be processed by the model API configured in MaiBot. Google, the search service, and the configured model provider process the information necessary to provide their respective services.

The integration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train a model. Use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on the operator’s server in a file restricted to its owner. Search queries, notes, and request identifiers are retained locally to track requests and prevent duplicate sending. Reply content becomes part of MaiBot’s tool results and may be retained in its conversation data. Emails remain subject to the Gmail account’s retention settings.

Control and removal

The account holder can revoke the integration’s access in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not automatically remove existing Gmail messages, local request records, or MaiBot conversation data. Contact the operator to request removal of locally retained data.
